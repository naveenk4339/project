Service and Maintenance

Electric vehicles need less maintenance than gasoline cars: there are no oil changes, spark plugs, or timing belts.

Recommended schedule:
- Tire rotation: every 7,500 miles
- Cabin air filter: every 2 years
- Brake fluid check: every 2 years
- Air-conditioning desiccant bag: every 6 years

Booking service: use the Northwind mobile app or call 1-800-555-0199. Mobile Service technicians can perform many repairs at the customer's home or workplace at no extra charge within 50 miles of a service center.

Roadside assistance is included for the duration of the Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty and covers towing to the nearest service center, flat-tire help, and lockout assistance.
