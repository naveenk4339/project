Returns, Financing and Leasing

7-day return policy: new vehicles may be returned within 7 days or 1,000 miles of delivery, whichever comes first, for a full refund, provided the vehicle is undamaged.

Financing: Northwind Financial offers loans from 36 to 72 months. Rates depend on credit history; promotional APR offers are announced quarterly on the website.

Leasing: 24- and 36-month leases are available on the Aurora and Summit with 10,000, 12,000 or 15,000 miles per year. The Ridge is not currently offered for lease.

Trade-ins: customers can receive an instant trade-in estimate in the online order flow. The final value is confirmed at inspection on delivery day.
