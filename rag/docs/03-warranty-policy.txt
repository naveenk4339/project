Northwind Warranty Policy

Basic Vehicle Limited Warranty: 4 years or 50,000 miles, whichever comes first. Covers defects in materials and workmanship for all parts supplied by Northwind, except items listed under exclusions.

Battery and Drive Unit Warranty: 8 years or 120,000 miles, whichever comes first. The battery is guaranteed to retain at least 70% of its original capacity during the warranty period.

Corrosion (perforation) Warranty: 12 years, unlimited miles.

Exclusions: tires, wiper blades, brake pads, and damage from accidents, misuse, flooding, or unauthorized modifications. Cosmetic wear is not covered.

Warranty coverage transfers automatically to subsequent owners at no cost.
