# Loyalty Program

## Earning
- Members earn 1 point per whole dollar spent before tax. GOLD members earn 2 points per dollar.
- Points are added a few seconds after the sale completes. Attach the customer to the cart *before* checkout -
  points cannot be added to a sale after it is completed.

## Tiers
- BRONZE: 0-499 lifetime points
- SILVER: 500-1,999 lifetime points
- GOLD: 2,000+ lifetime points
Tiers are based on lifetime points earned, so refunds lower the spendable balance but not the tier.

## Refunds
Refunding a transaction automatically reverses exactly the points that transaction earned.

## Looking up a member
Ask for the phone number or scan the member card; the till shows the customer id, balance and tier. Never read a
member's details aloud.
