# Inventory & Receiving

## Stock levels
Stock is decremented automatically a few seconds after each completed sale and restored by refunds. The low-stock
report lists SKUs at or below their reorder point.

## Receiving a delivery
1. Check the delivery against the packing slip.
2. In **Inventory > Adjust**, enter the SKU and the positive quantity received with reason "delivery".
3. Report shortages or damaged cases to the store manager the same day.

## Shrink
Record damaged, expired or stolen stock as a negative adjustment with the reason "shrink".

## Reordering
Use the demand forecast (Inventory > Forecast) to size orders: order enough to cover the forecast for the next
7 days plus the reorder point, minus what is on hand.
