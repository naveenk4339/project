# Refunds & Returns

## Who can refund
- Refunds up to $100.00 can be processed by any associate.
- Refunds over $100.00, and any refund without a receipt, need a supervisor PIN on the till.
- Associates cannot refund their own purchases.

## Time limits
- Most items: 30 days from purchase with receipt.
- Electronics (category `electronics`): 15 days, and the item must be unopened or defective.
- Bakery and beverages are not returnable unless the product was wrong or defective.

## How to refund at the till
1. Find the sale: scan the barcode on the receipt, or look it up by transaction id (TX-YYYYMMDD-XXXXXXXX) under **Transactions**.
2. Confirm the transaction status is COMPLETED. A transaction that is already REFUNDED cannot be refunded again.
3. Choose **Refund**, enter the reason, and confirm. The system refunds the full transaction to the original tender:
   - card payments go back to the same card (allow 3-5 business days for the customer to see it);
   - cash payments are paid out from the drawer.
4. The refund updates inventory and reverses any loyalty points the sale earned, automatically.
5. Offer the customer the refunded receipt (it is marked *** REFUNDED ***).

## Partial returns
The till refunds whole transactions. For a partial return, refund the transaction and ring up the items the
customer is keeping as a new sale; promotions are re-evaluated on the new basket.

## No receipt
Look the sale up by date and approximate time with **Transactions > Recent**. If it cannot be found, a supervisor
may issue store credit at the lowest selling price in the last 30 days.
