# Promotions & Pricing

Promotions are applied automatically by the pricing service when the basket is priced; associates never key in
discounts for these.

## Current promotions
- **BAKERY-20** - 20% off every bakery item (muffins, croissants).
- **CHIPS-B2G1** - Buy 2 Sea Salt Chips, get the 3rd free. Applies per group of three in the same basket.
- **SPEND50-5** - $5.00 off when the basket reaches $50.00 after item-level discounts. The $5.00 is spread across
  the lines in proportion to their value, so a later refund returns the right amount.

## Order of application
Item promotions (category percentage, buy-X-get-Y) are applied first, then order-threshold discounts, then sales
tax on taxable items only. Beverages and bakery items are not taxable; snacks, electronics and merchandise are.

## Tax rates
- store-001 (Michigan): 6%
- store-002 (California): 7.25%

## Price overrides
Price overrides need a supervisor PIN and a reason code (damaged, price match, manager discretion). Price matches
are only against a current printed or on-screen ad from a local competitor.
