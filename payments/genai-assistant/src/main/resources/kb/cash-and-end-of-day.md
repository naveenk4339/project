# Cash Handling & End of Day

## Opening
Each drawer starts with a $150.00 float: 4x$20, 4x$10, 4x$5, 10x$1, plus $10.00 in coins. Count it with a second
associate and sign the float sheet.

## During the shift
- Keep no more than $400.00 in the drawer; drop the excess into the safe with a drop slip.
- Count change back to the customer starting from the total.
- Check $50 and $100 notes with the counterfeit pen.

## End of day
1. Run **Reports > Sales summary** for the store. It shows gross and net sales, number of tickets, average ticket,
   refunds and the split by tender (CARD / CASH).
2. Count the drawer. Expected cash = float + CASH sales - cash refunds - drops.
3. Variances over $5.00 must be reported to the store manager on the variance log.
